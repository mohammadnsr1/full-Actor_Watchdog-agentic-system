---

Begin your role. You have the requirements before anything moves, and the
controller does not start until you signal ready: read them, work out which
invariants must be watched for the whole episode and which acceptance
contracts fall due at the end, fetch each part's contract once and keep it,
think through the physical events each part can go through, put the standing
monitors and wake conditions in place, and then signal ready. You are not told
what the robot will do, in what order, or when; from then on the cell's
physical events and your own observations are how you learn where things
stand, and they are what wakes you.

Record a finding the moment you have one. At the end of the episode, inspect
acceptance from your own observations and record the episode's single final
verdict, whether or not you recorded findings along the way.
